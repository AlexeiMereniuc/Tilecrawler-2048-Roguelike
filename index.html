English

Tilecrawler does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Your game progress, records and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this local game data in your own device backup in your Google account. This is controlled by Android and by you; the developer has no access to it.
  • The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. The internet permission comes with the app framework and is not used to send data.
  • The vibration permission is used only for haptic feedback in the game; you can turn vibration off in the game settings.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: alexei.mereniuc@outlook.com.
